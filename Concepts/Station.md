# Station

## Definition
An intermediate milestone on the way to a [[Goal]] — a "stop" on the [[Roadmap]]. A goal's plan is an ordered sequence of stations generated by [[AI Plan Generation]].

## Rules
- Stored as a [[Task]] with `type` ≠ `daily`.
- The current station is the first station with status `pending` (ordered by `generatedAt`).
- Station progress (`Goal.currentStationProgress`, 0–100) accumulates from `progressContribution` of completed [[Daily Task|daily tasks]].
- When progress reaches 100, the station is marked `done`, progress resets to 0 and the next station becomes active.

## Implemented in
[[Task Module]] (`/stations/:stationTitle/progress`), [[AI Module]] (`getProgressInfo`), [[Roadmap View]].
